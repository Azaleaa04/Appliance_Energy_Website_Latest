Television Energy Consumption Insights

Explore our findings on television models currently available on the Australian market, detailing screen technologies, brand distributions, and power scaling.

1. Screen Technologies Breakdown

LCD (LED) models make up the vast majority of available televisions in Australia, offering a balance of manufacturing cost and energy efficiency compared to standard LCD and high-end OLED displays.

2. Screen Size vs Power Consumption

Our exploratory scatter plots demonstrate a clear positive correlation: as screen sizes scale up in inches, operating mode power consumption increases correspondingly.
