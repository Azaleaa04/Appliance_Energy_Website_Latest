Television Energy Consumption Insights

Explore our data-driven exploration and visual findings for televisions currently available on the Australian market.

1. What type of TV screen technologies are currently available in Australia and which are the most frequent?

Finding: There are 3 types of screen technologies are currently available in Australia which are OLED, LCD and LCD(LED). The most frequent screen technology available on the Australian market is LCD(LED).

2. What screen sizes are currently available, and which are the most frequent?

Finding: The television screen sizes that are available and most frequent is 65.0 inches.

3. Which brands have the greatest number of different models?

Finding: Kogan leads the market with the highest number of registered models,followed by LG and Samsung Electronics.

4. Which type of screen technology consumes the least amount of power?

Finding: Standard LCD displays consume the least amount of median operating power

5. What is the relationship between screen size and power use?

Finding: There is a strong positive correlation. As screen size increases, the average mode power consumption also increases correspondingly.

6. What is the relationship between star rating and screen size?

Finding: There is no relationship.

7. Are there differences in power consumption between brands?

Finding: Yes, there are clear differences in average power consumption whereby operating power demands vary noticeably across different television manufacturers.
