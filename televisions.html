Television Energy Consumption Insights (Australia)

Explore our data-driven exploration of televisions currently available on the Australian market, detailing screen technologies, brand distributions, and power scaling.

1. Screen Technologies Breakdown

Finding: LCD (LED) models dominate the Australian market by a wide margin (~4,080 models), offering an optimal balance of manufacturing cost and energy efficiency compared to standard LCD (~603 models) and premium OLED displays (~321 models).

2. Top Brands by Model Count

Finding: Major global manufacturers such as LG, Samsung, and Kogan account for the highest volume of registered television models available to consumers.

3. Screen Size vs. Power Consumption

Finding: Our KNIME scatter plot analysis proves a clear positive correlation. As screen dimensions scale up in size, the operating mode power consumption increases correspondingly.
