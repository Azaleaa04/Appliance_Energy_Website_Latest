Television Energy Consumption Insights

Explore our data-driven exploration and visual findings for televisions currently available on the Australian market.

1. What type of TV screen technologies are currently available in Australia and which are the most frequent?

Finding: LCD (LED) is by far the most frequent screen technology available on the Australian market (~4,080 models), followed by standard LCD (~603 models) and OLED (~321 models).

2. What screen sizes are currently available, and which are the most frequent?

Finding: Screen sizes range widely from small portable displays up to large home-theater screens, with popular standard sizes forming clear frequency peaks across the distribution.

3. Which brands have the greatest number of different models?

Finding: Major manufacturers such as Kogan, LG, and Samsung lead the market with the highest volume of registered television models.

4. Which type of screen technology consumes the least amount of power?

Finding: Standard LCD displays generally consume the lowest median operating power (~71.6W), whereas LCD (LED) and OLED have higher median power requirements depending on brightness and screen dimensions.

5. What is the relationship between screen size and power use?

Finding: There is a strong positive correlation. As screen size (in inches) increases, the average mode power consumption (watts) scales up correspondingly.

6. What is the relationship between star rating and screen size?

Finding: Energy efficiency star ratings distribute across various screen sizes, showing how larger displays attempt to maintain energy standards despite higher power draw.

7. Are there differences in power consumption between brands?

Finding: Comparing median operating power across top brands highlights noticeable variations in energy efficiency profiles among manufacturers.
